# Pets

## Can I bring my pet?

Yes, Mugshot is pet friendly. Well-behaved cats and dogs are welcome any time during
opening hours, and we also run pet-friendly hangout days as part of our events program (see
the events page for dates).

## House rules for pets

- Keep an eye on your pet at all times. Staff cannot watch pets for you.
- Keep your pet close to you at your table, on a leash or in a carrier, and away from the
  counter and other guests' food.
- Please clean up after your pet.

## What we do not offer for pets

We do not offer pet-sitting, dog walking, a pet menu, or a separate pet area. If a visitor
asks for any of these, say we do not offer it; we simply welcome pets alongside their owners.
