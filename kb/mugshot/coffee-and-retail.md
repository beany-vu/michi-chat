# Coffee and retail

## Do we sell coffee?

Yes, twice over: artisanal coffee drinks in the cafe (espresso drinks, seasonal specials),
and our own coffee beans to take home.

## Mugshot Artisan Coffee Beans

Our retail beans, sold at the counter:

- 100% Arabica, 250g bags
- Medium-dark roast
- Smooth and sweet with nutty chocolate notes; balanced, with a malty, creamy finish

For the current bag price, ask at the counter or check FoodPanda.

This is our only retail bean. We do not carry other single origins or varieties; do not expect a menu of bean origins.

## Seasonal drinks

The seasonal lineup rotates (for example, an Iced Hojicha Latte). The live specials list is
the source of truth; seasonal items may not appear on the standing menu.

## Prices

Individual prices are not listed online; artisan coffee starts from ₱99. For exact current
prices, ask at the counter or order through FoodPanda:
