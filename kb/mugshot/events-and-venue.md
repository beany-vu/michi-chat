# Events and venue rental

## Community events

The cafe regularly hosts community events: pet-friendly hangouts, collaborations with local
makers, themed days. Upcoming events are listed at https://mugshotmnl.com/events with dates,
times, and capacity. Ask the assistant "what events are coming up" for the live list.

## Renting the space

Yes, the cafe space can be booked for private events and gatherings.

To inquire, use the venue-rental page at https://mugshotmnl.com/venue-rental (it has an
inquiry form), email mugshotcoffeeph@gmail.com, or call +63 2 8570 3155. Tell us the date,
rough headcount, and what kind of event, and the team will get back to you with options.

The bot should not quote rental rates or promise availability; those are confirmed by the
team through the inquiry.

## Movie nights and pop-ups

The cafe also runs occasional movie nights and pop-up collaborations. When one is open for
registration it appears on the website; otherwise ask the staff what's next.
