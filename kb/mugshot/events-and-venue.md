# Events and venue rental

## Community events and workshops

The cafe runs a regular program of events: hands-on workshops (pottery and clay - including
build-your-own-mug sessions, flower arranging, resin art, hand weaving, painting nights),
relaxed art sessions, and community days such as pet-friendly hangouts and seasonal
celebrations. Workshops are intimate, typically 5 to 30 seats.

Upcoming events are listed at https://mugshotmnl.com/events with dates, times, and capacity;
ask the assistant "what events are coming up" for the live list. Some events take
registrations through a sign-up link on their event page; where there is no link, message us
or ask the staff to reserve a slot.

## Renting the space

Yes - Mugshot is an intimate venue with a speaker and projector, seating for up to 20 guests,
suited to birthdays, small meetings, private film showings, workshops, celebrations and
other private gatherings. Custom catering can be arranged.

The event rates and packages are published at https://mugshotmnl.com/pages/venue-rental/
(see the next section). To book, use the event inquiry form on the website: on the home page
https://mugshotmnl.com, scroll to "Host your event at Mugshot" and press "Inquire about the
space" (the same form is on the events page). It asks for your name, email, event date, event
type and number of guests, and the team replies within 24 hours. Email
mugshotcoffeeph@gmail.com or Instagram @mugshotcoffeeph also work. Phone +63 2 8570 3155
exists but is not always answered. The team confirms the date and availability; the bot
cannot book or hold a date.

## Event rates and packages

Three packages, all for 3 hours of exclusive use of the whole cafe, with extra time at
₱500 per hour:

- Venue Rental, ₱3,500: exclusive shop rental for 3 hours, includes ₱2,500 consumable on
  food and drinks, extension ₱500 per hour.
- Celebration Package, ₱4,500: coffee and non-coffee drinks for 10 guests, one whole cake or
  10 pasta servings of your choice, popcorn good for 10 people, free use of the speaker and
  projector, 3 hours exclusive use, extension ₱500 per hour.
- Full Package, ₱5,500: coffee and non-coffee drinks for 10 guests, one whole cake, 10 pasta
  servings of your choice, popcorn good for 10 people, free use of the speaker and projector,
  3 hours exclusive use, extension ₱500 per hour.

These are the only published rates. There is no other package, no half-day or full-day rate,
and no per-head price; for anything not listed (more guests, longer hours, outside food,
decorations), ask the team through the inquiry and they will quote. Rates can change, so the
venue-rental page is the source of truth.

## Coffee cart pop-ups

We also bring the cafe to you: a mobile coffee cart with our baristas serving freshly brewed
specialty coffee at parties, weddings, corporate events, and other occasions. Details are at
https://mugshotmnl.com/pages/cafe-popup/.

Pop-up packages, priced by number of cups:

- 50 cups: ₱8,000
- 100 cups: ₱14,000
- 150 cups: ₱22,000

Prices include the logistics fee within a 5 km radius of the cafe. Every package includes
4 to 6 hours on site (including setup and teardown), the full coffee cart setup, 2 baristas,
single-origin beans from Brazil for the cart, espresso and cold brew options, ceremonial
grade matcha, high quality tea, and a high-end machine and grinder.

Terms: a 50 percent downpayment confirms the booking; extra cups are ₱120 per cup; the venue
must provide a main outlet with a 20 amp supply; time beyond the allotted hours is ₱1,000 per
hour. Events farther than 5 km, other cup counts, or special menus are quoted by the team.
Book through the event inquiry form, email, or Instagram with your date, location and
expected guest count.

The Brazil single-origin beans are what the pop-up cart brews at events; they are not a
retail bean. The only bean sold to take home is Mugshot Artisan Coffee Beans.

## Art at the cafe

Mugshot doubles as an artisan space: local art curated from artist friends hangs in the cafe,
and creative workshops are part of the regular program.

## The Mugshot experience: art, coffee and community

Mugshot is as much a creative space as a cafe. Local art from artist friends is curated on
the walls, and the events program is hands-on and social: people come to make something,
not just to watch. Recent and regular formats include pottery and clay sessions (including
build-your-own-mug), flower arranging, resin art, hand weaving, painting and doodle nights,
and community days like pet-friendly hangouts. Groups are small, so a workshop feels like a
gathering of friends rather than a class.

If a visitor wants to support local artists, join a creative night, or bring a group for a
private session, point them to the upcoming events list and the venue-rental inquiry. Ask
the assistant "what workshops are coming up" for the live schedule.
