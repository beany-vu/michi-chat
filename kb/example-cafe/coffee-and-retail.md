# Coffee and retail

Invented sample data for the michi-chat demo tenant.

## The coffee

Yes, Example Cafe is a coffee shop first. The espresso bar serves the usual range:
espresso, americano, latte, cappuccino, flat white, cold brew, and a house mocha. There is
a non-coffee list too: matcha, chocolate, and fruit sodas.

Drinks start at 110 pesos for a single-shot americano. A latte is 150 pesos hot or 165
pesos iced. Adding an extra shot is 30 pesos.

## Best seller

The Salted Caramel Cold Brew is the runaway favourite, followed by the plain flat white.
Staff will usually point a first-time visitor at one of those two.

## Beans to take home

Whole beans and ground coffee are sold at the counter in 250 gram bags, 480 pesos each.
Three options are usually in stock:

- House Blend, a medium roast, chocolate and toasted nut, the same beans used on the bar
- Morning Light, a light roast single origin, citrus and floral, best as filter
- Deep Lane, a dark roast, heavy and smoky, made for milk drinks

Ask staff to grind a bag for your brewer. Beans are roasted locally in small batches and
bagged with a roast date.

## Seasonal drinks

The specials board changes every few months: a calamansi cold brew tonic in summer, a spiced
pear latte in the cooler months, and a rotating guest single origin on filter. The live
specials list is on the cafe's website, so ask the assistant for what is on right now rather
than relying on this document.

## Merchandise

Mugs, tote bags, and a small selection of brewing gear (paper filters, a hand grinder, and
two pour-over drippers) are on the retail shelf.
