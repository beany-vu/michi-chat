# Events and venue rental

Invented sample data for the michi-chat demo tenant.

## Events at the cafe

Yes, the cafe runs a regular events program: hands-on workshops (pottery and clay, including
build-your-own-mug sessions, plus flower arranging and painting nights), acoustic music
nights, and a monthly movie night on the last Friday. Workshops are small, usually 6 to 24
seats.

Upcoming dates, times and capacity are published on the cafe's website, and the assistant
can read the live list, so ask "what events are coming up" rather than trusting a date
written in this document. Some events take sign-ups through a link on their event page;
where there is none, message the cafe to reserve a slot.

## Renting the space

The whole cafe can be booked for private use: birthdays, small meetings, private screenings,
workshops, and team gatherings. It seats up to 24 guests comfortably, or about 35 standing.
A projector, a screen, and a small speaker system are included at no extra cost.

To book, use the enquiry form on the cafe's website, or email hello@example-cafe.test with
your date, guest count, and what you have in mind. The team replies within two working days
and confirms availability. The assistant cannot hold or book a date.

## Venue rates

All packages cover three hours of exclusive use of the whole cafe. Extra time is 600 pesos
per hour.

- Basic Rental, 4,000 pesos: exclusive use for three hours, includes 3,000 pesos of
  consumable credit on food and drinks.
- Celebration Package, 5,200 pesos: drinks for 10 guests, one whole cake or 10 pasta
  servings, popcorn for 10, use of the projector and speakers, three hours exclusive use.
- Full Package, 6,500 pesos: drinks for 15 guests, one whole cake, 15 pasta servings,
  popcorn, projector and speakers, three hours exclusive use, plus simple table styling.

Custom catering can be arranged with two weeks notice.

## Coffee cart for offsite events

Yes, the cafe sends a coffee cart to offices, weddings, and market days within Metro Manila.
The cart comes with one barista, a two-group espresso machine, cups, and a menu of four
drinks.

Pricing is per cup with a minimum booking:

- 50 cups, 12,000 pesos, about two hours of service
- 100 cups, 21,000 pesos, about three hours of service
- Above 100 cups, quoted per event

Travel outside Metro Manila is charged separately. Book at least three weeks ahead, since
the cart is a single unit and dates go quickly.
