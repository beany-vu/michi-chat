# Hours and location

Example Cafe is a small neighbourhood coffee shop. Everything in this document is invented
sample data for the michi-chat demo tenant; it does not describe a real business.

## Opening hours

Open 8:00 in the morning until 9:00 at night, every day except Tuesday, when the cafe is
closed for cleaning and staff training. The kitchen stops taking hot food orders 30 minutes
before closing.

Public holidays follow the normal schedule unless announced on the cafe's social accounts.

## Where we are

12 Sample Street, Barangay Testville, Quezon City, Metro Manila.

The cafe sits on the ground floor of a two-storey building, next to a hardware shop. Look
for the green awning.

## Parking and Wi-Fi

There is free parking for six cars in the small lot behind the building, plus street
parking along Sample Street. Free Wi-Fi is available for customers; ask the counter for the
password.
