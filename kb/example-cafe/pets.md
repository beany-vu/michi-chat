# Pets

Invented sample data for the michi-chat demo tenant.

Well-behaved dogs are welcome in the outdoor seating area at the front of the cafe, on a
lead at all times. Water bowls are available on request.

Dogs are not allowed in the indoor dining area, apart from assistance dogs, which are
welcome everywhere in the cafe.

Cats and other pets are welcome in the outdoor area too, in a carrier or on a harness.

Owners are responsible for their pets, and staff may ask that a pet be moved outside if it
disturbs other guests.
