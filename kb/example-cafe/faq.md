# Frequently asked questions

Invented sample data for the michi-chat demo tenant.

## How much is a latte?

150 pesos hot, 165 pesos iced. An extra shot is 30 pesos. The full drink list is in the
coffee document.

## Is your coffee any good?

The house espresso is a medium roast blend, roasted locally in small batches and dialled in
daily. Regulars come back for the Salted Caramel Cold Brew and the flat white. Tastes
differ, so the staff are happy to make a recommendation or let you smell the beans before
you buy a bag.

## How can I pay? Is GCash okay?

Cash, all major cards, GCash, and Maya are accepted. There is no minimum spend for card
payments. Bank transfer is accepted for venue bookings and coffee cart deposits.

## Do you deliver?

Delivery inside Quezon City and nearby areas runs through Foodpanda and Grab Food. Foodpanda
is a food delivery app: search for Example Cafe in the app and order from the same menu
served in store. Delivery to Makati is possible when a rider accepts the distance, but it is
outside the usual range and can be slow, so it is not recommended for hot drinks.

Large office orders can be arranged directly with the cafe instead of through an app.

## Can I reserve a table?

Tables for up to 6 guests are first come, first served and cannot be reserved. Groups of 7
or more can be reserved with at least one day of notice, by phone or email, and are seated
at the long table at the back. To take over the whole cafe, see the venue rental document.

## Do you have vegan food? I have a nut allergy.

There are vegan options: oat milk at no extra cost, two vegan sandwiches, and a vegan
banana bread. The kitchen is small and handles nuts, sesame, dairy, and gluten on shared
surfaces, so cross-contact cannot be ruled out. Tell the staff about an allergy when
ordering and they will tell you honestly what is safe and what is not.

## Is there parking and Wi-Fi?

Yes to both. Free parking for six cars behind the building, plus street parking. Free
customer Wi-Fi; ask the counter for the password.

## Do you have any discounts or promos?

There is a loyalty card: buy nine drinks, get the tenth free. Students get 10 percent off
before noon on weekdays with a valid ID. Seasonal promos are announced on the cafe's
Instagram rather than here, since this document does not change often.
