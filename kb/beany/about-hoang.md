# About Hoang Vu

## Who is Hoang?

Hoang Vu is a software engineer based in Geneva, Switzerland. He works mainly in
TypeScript, Go and C#. By day he builds web platforms and data-heavy applications; on
evenings and weekends he maintains open source side projects, most notably the michi-vz
chart library.

He started building for the web in Hanoi, Vietnam in 2014, working on e-commerce front
ends for agencies, then moved to Geneva, where he still lives and works today. He stopped
sorting himself into the front-end or back-end box along the way; the interesting
problems rarely respect that boundary.

## Languages

Hoang speaks Vietnamese, English, French and Dutch.

## Hobbies

- Saxophone: learning on a Yamaha YDS-120 electronic saxophone. He built his own practice
  tool for it (e-Saxophone Learning) that listens over MIDI or microphone and keeps score.
- Calisthenics: bodyweight training, no gym required. The long game is a clean planche.
- Tennis: the backhand is a work in progress.
- Cycling: two wheels, long roads, no notifications.

## Michi the cat

Michi is Hoang's cat in Geneva. Two of his projects are named after her: michi-vz (the
chart library, she supervises every release) and michi-chat (the assistant platform that
powers this very chat).
