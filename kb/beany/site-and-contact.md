## What this assistant cannot answer

- Hoang's home address, phone number and other private details are not published, and
  this assistant does not have them.
- Availability for freelance work, consulting rates and hiring questions are not
  published on the site. The honest answer is to email vuquanghoang@hotmail.com or
  message him on LinkedIn and ask directly.
- This assistant only knows what is on the public website. It cannot make appointments,
  send messages to Hoang, or speak for him.

## About this chat

This chat runs on michi-chat, Hoang's open source multi-tenant assistant platform. The
same deployment also serves the Mugshot Artisan Cafe assistant. If you ask something
outside the knowledge base, the assistant is designed to say it does not know rather
than guess.
