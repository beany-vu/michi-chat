Meet your assistant ​

This is what your customers see: a chat on your website that answers questions about your business - opening hours, the menu, delivery, policies - in your voice, any time of day.

A few things to notice in that picture:

  • "Checking our info" - the assistant is looking the answer up in your knowledge base (facts you wrote), not making something up. If it can't find an answer, it says so and points people to your staff.
  • It never invents prices or availability. If you don't publish prices, it directs customers to the counter or your delivery app - that behavior is built in.
  • The small notice at the bottom asks visitors not to share personal or sensitive information, and explains that the assistant only handles questions about your business. You write that text yourself, so it can match your tone (and your local privacy rules).

What it will and won't do ​

It willIt won't
Answer questions using facts you providedGuess when it doesn't know
Suggest items from your live menuQuote prices you haven't published
Stay polite when someone isn'tPlay along with abuse or off-topic requests
Work at 3 AMPromise callbacks or take actions you haven't enabled

What running it looks like ​

You manage everything from one admin page - no coding: what the assistant knows, how it sounds, which websites it appears on, and a daily message limit that caps what a day of chat can ever cost you.
