Any model, by config

The app only knows three aliases (michi, judge, embed). LiteLLM decides what serves them, so moving from local Ollama to a hosted provider is a yaml edit.

Built for strangers

The public endpoint assumes untrusted traffic. Origin allowlists, server-minted sessions, rate limits and a hard daily cap protect the bill against anyone with curl.

Grounded answers

A pgvector knowledge base with heading-aware chunking and a measured recall@k eval. The bot says "I don't know" instead of inventing facts or prices.
